Questions, answered

Why is every package priced as "starting at"?

Because page count, integrations, and how many channels you run all change the real number, and pretending otherwise produces padded quotes. The starting prices are honest floors. One call places you in the right package with a fixed number in writing.

Can I start with a website and add marketing later?

Yes, and it is a common path. The Website package is built so marketing can be layered on top of it without a rebuild: tracking, page structure, and SEO groundwork are already in place when you move up.

Do you work on fixed price or hourly?

Fixed price for projects, flat retainers for ongoing work. Hourly billing rewards slowness, so we do not use it.

What does the payment schedule look like?

Projects are typically split into a deposit to book the build and milestones tied to delivery. Retainers bill monthly. Everything is in writing before work starts.

What if none of the packages fit?

Then we build a custom plan around your goals, budget, and growth stage. Plenty of businesses need one piece rather than a whole tier. If your budget and your goal do not match, we will say so on the first call and tell you what to do instead.

Is there a charge for the initial audit?

The first call and the scoping that follows it are free. The full week one audit with a prioritized fix list is part of every paid engagement, as described in our process.
