Questions, answered

Do you work with Shopify?

Yes. We design and optimize storefronts on the platform you run, and our marquee includes brands built around Shopify operations.

Can you fix our rising customer acquisition costs?

Usually by rebalancing the mix: sharper creative to lift paid efficiency, SEO and content to add owned traffic, and conversion work so every visit is worth more. The blend is what moves blended CAC.

How fast does ecommerce SEO show results?

Our benchmark DTC result was 312 percent organic growth inside 90 days. Every store differs, but category and content architecture usually show movement within the first quarter.

Do you produce the ad creative too?

Yes. Hooks, statics, and short form video ship from our team weekly, because on Meta the creative is the targeting.

We are a service business for ecommerce, not a store. Fit?

Perfect fit. Ecom Heroes is exactly that: a remote staffing partner for ecommerce brands whose identity and website we built end to end.
