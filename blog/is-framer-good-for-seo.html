Is Framer good for SEO?

The direct answer from a studio that ships ranking Framer sites: what the platform handles, what still needs a human, and the checklist we launch with.

The direct answer

Yes. Framer is good for SEO. Sites built on Framer can and do rank on the first page of Google, because Framer handles the technical layer that most platforms get wrong: clean semantic HTML, fast edge hosting, automatic sitemaps, editable metadata, canonical tags, redirects, and image optimization. The question that actually matters is different: SEO is decided mostly by strategy, content, and authority. Framer gives you a fast, technically clean stage. What you perform on it is still up to you, or to your SEO team.

What Framer handles for you

  • Performance. Framer publishes static pages to a global CDN. With sane design choices, Core Web Vitals land in the green on mobile, which is table stakes for competitive rankings.
  • Clean markup. Headings, paragraphs, and links render as real semantic HTML that crawlers parse without executing a JavaScript obstacle course.
  • Metadata control. Per page titles, descriptions, social images, and canonical URLs, all editable without code.
  • Sitemaps and robots. Generated and updated automatically as pages change.
  • Redirects. Full 301 support, which is what makes safe migrations and restructures possible.
  • Responsive rendering. Real responsive breakpoints rather than a separate mobile site, matching how Google indexes mobile first.

What is still your job

No platform researches your keywords, maps them to pages, writes copy that answers what buyers actually search, earns links, or builds the internal linking that turns pages into a system. Those are the inputs that decide whether a technically clean site ranks for anything worth ranking for. A Framer site with no strategy will sit politely unread, exactly like a WordPress site with no strategy, just faster.

Platforms do not rank. Pages rank. Framer just makes sure the page starts with no technical handicaps.

The launch checklist we run on every Framer site

  • One target search intent per page, mapped before design starts
  • Unique title under 60 characters and description under 160 on every page
  • One h1 per page with a logical h2 and h3 outline underneath
  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful images, empty alt on decorative ones
  • Organization, breadcrumb, and FAQ structured data where they apply
  • Internal links with descriptive anchors connecting services, proof, and posts
  • 301 redirects mapped for any URL that changed from a previous site
  • Search Console verified, sitemap submitted, coverage checked at day 7

Honest limitations to know

Framer is young compared to WordPress, so some enterprise SEO edge cases, like elaborate hreflang setups across dozens of locales or programmatic pages generated from massive datasets, take more thought or fit better elsewhere. For the marketing sites most businesses need, from five to fifty pages, none of these limits bite. Every Framer case study on our work page launched with the checklist above and clean technical audits.

Verdict

Framer is not just acceptable for SEO. For marketing sites it is one of the best foundations available right now: technically clean by default, fast by default, and easy to keep publishing on, which is the part of SEO most businesses actually fail at. Pair it with a real strategy and the rankings follow the same rules they always have. If you want both halves handled, our Framer web design and SEO services are built to run together.

Questions, answered

Can Framer websites rank on page one of Google?

Yes. Framer outputs fast, clean, crawlable pages with full metadata control. Rankings then depend on your keyword strategy, content quality, and authority, the same as on any platform.

Does Framer generate a sitemap automatically?

Yes. Sitemaps and robots directives are handled automatically and update as your pages change, and you can submit the sitemap to Google Search Console as usual.

Is WordPress better than Framer for SEO?

Not inherently. WordPress offers plugin depth for edge cases, but for typical marketing sites Framer matches the fundamentals with better speed and far less maintenance. Strategy decides the winner, not the CMS.

Do I need an SEO plugin on Framer?

No. The controls that plugins add to WordPress, titles, descriptions, canonicals, redirects, sitemaps, are built into Framer natively.
