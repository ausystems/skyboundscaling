Process · Four phases · One rail

Four phases,one rail.

Every Skybound engagement, from a Framer website to a full growth retainer, runs the same operating system: audit what exists, map the strategy, build fast, then scale what the numbers prove. Here is exactly what happens and when.

The four phases

Audit to scale

01

Phase 01 · Audit

Week one. We pull apart what is already running: site, ads, funnels, brand. You get a prioritized fix list with a dollar value on every gap, so the plan starts from your numbers, not our assumptions. Deliverables: technical audit, conversion review, channel scorecard.

02

Phase 02 · Strategy

Weeks two and three. We map the architecture: channels, creative angles, offer stack, landing pages. Every asset gets a job and a number it has to hit before it ships. Deliverables: growth map, messaging framework, measurement plan.

03

Phase 03 · Build

Weeks three to six. Design, write, ship. New site, new ad sets, new content engine, every release live and measured from day one. You see the numbers before we do. Deliverables: the build itself, tracking, launch checklist.

04

Phase 04 · Scale

Ongoing. Winners get more budget, losers get cut, and the system sharpens every week. This is where most agencies tap out and where the compounding actually happens. Deliverables: weekly optimization, monthly revenue reporting.

Why it is built this way

Agencies stall projects with process theatre. Ours exists to remove decisions, not add meetings.

Each phase ends with something shipped, not something presented. The audit ships fixes, the strategy ships a map with numbers attached, the build ships the asset, and scale ships improvements weekly. If a step does not move the work forward, it is not in the process.

It also means you always know where you stand. One senior owns your engagement end to end, the reporting reads like a P and L, and there is never a week where you wonder what is happening. That is what working with a Toronto web design and marketing team should feel like.

Questions, answered

How long does a typical engagement take?

A focused website launches in three to six weeks from brief. Brand plus site systems run six to nine weeks. Growth retainers are ongoing, with the first revenue report inside the first month.

Who do we actually work with day to day?

The senior who scoped your project. One point of contact, on the tools, answering inside one business day.

What do you need from us to start?

Access to what exists: site, analytics, ad accounts, brand files. Then one working session to align on the goal. We handle the rest and tell you exactly what content to prepare.

When do results show up?

Fixes from the week one audit often move numbers immediately. Paid channels read out inside weeks, SEO compounds over months, and every engagement reports monthly so progress is never a mystery.
