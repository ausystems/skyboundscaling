The story

We started Skybound in 2025 because we kept watching the same movie. Brands paying agency prices for junior work.

The pitch meeting has the partners in it. The project has the interns. Somewhere between signature and kickoff, the seniors vanish and the deck gets handed down the ladder. We built Skybound to be the opposite machine: a small Toronto team where the strategist on the call is the designer in the file and the operator in the ad account.

We began in 2024 as AuDesigns, a web design and branding studio, and rebranded as Skybound Scaling in 2025. Two years in, the model holds. Thirty eight brands shipped and scaled, forty two million dollars in tracked client revenue, and a client wall that runs from luxury home builders to healthcare AI. Not because we are the biggest agency in Toronto, but because we refuse the one habit that makes agencies big and slow: selling hours instead of outcomes.
