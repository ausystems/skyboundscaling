Questions, answered

Do Facebook ads work for contractors in Vaughan?

Exceptionally, when the creative is real project work. Homeowners plan visually and early; the contractor in their feed for months wins the estimate request when the project becomes real.

What creative do I need to provide?

Job site photos and permission to film. We handle the rest: editing, hooks, and copy. A phone walkthrough of a finished backyard routinely outperforms polished studio ads.

How do you measure success beyond likes?

Estimate requests, calls, and booked jobs, tracked from ad to CRM. Engagement is a byproduct; the report reads in appointments and revenue.

Meta ads or Google Ads first for a Vaughan contractor?

Google first if you need calls this month, Meta first if your pipeline planning horizon is seasonal. Most established contractors here end up running Google for capture and Meta for demand creation.
