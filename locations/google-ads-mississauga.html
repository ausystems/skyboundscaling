The market

Mississauga searches like a procurement office. Specific services, specific specs, and a vendor list at the end.

B2B and industrial categories dominate paid search here: freight and 3PL terms, contract manufacturing, equipment, staffing, and corporate services. Click prices are moderate compared to downtown Toronto consumer terms, but the buyers are worth far more, and they search with intent phrases most accounts never structure for: near Pearson, same day, ISO certified, by the pallet.

That is the opportunity. Campaigns built around service plus specification, with landing pages that answer like a capabilities deck, quietly win categories here while competitors bid generic head terms and burn budget on mismatched clicks.

Questions, answered

How much should a Mississauga B2B company spend on Google Ads?

Enough to exit the learning phase in your category, typically a few thousand dollars monthly to start. B2B clicks here cost less than downtown consumer terms, and one landed account often pays the year.

Do Google Ads work for logistics and industrial companies?

Very well, because the searches are specific and urgent: a shipper needing capacity or a plant needing a supplier searches with intent. The work is matching campaign structure to specification language, which is where we live.

Can you track leads that close by phone and email?

Yes. Call tracking plus offline conversion imports tie phoned and invoiced deals back to the exact keyword, which is the only honest way to run B2B search.

Why are my current Mississauga campaigns not converting?

The usual finds: generic head terms, no negative discipline, and clicks landing on a homepage instead of a capability page. Our week one audit prices each leak before we touch bids.
