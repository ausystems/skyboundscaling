The market

Markham is where launches live or die on the feed. New products need demand creation, and demand creation is a Meta job.

For the startup ecosystem here, the sequence matters: before anyone searches for your product category, they have to learn it exists. Meta creates that demand with launch creative, waitlist campaigns, and founder story angles, then retargeting holds attention through the evaluation weeks that follow. It is the same architecture we wired for the VAZA pre launch funnel, applied to a Markham roadmap.

The consumer side of Markham adds a dimension most agencies ignore: one of Canada’s most multilingual audiences. Creative that lands here is visual first and culturally aware, tested across communities rather than translated as an afterthought. The feed rewards brands that actually look like the city.

Questions, answered

Do Meta ads work for tech startups in Markham?

They are usually the first profitable channel, because new categories have no search volume to capture. Launch creative plus retargeting builds the demand Google later harvests.

Can you create ads for multilingual audiences?

Yes. We design visual first creative and structure campaigns so winning angles localize cleanly across Markham’s communities rather than shipping one translated afterthought.

What does a startup Meta budget look like?

Lean but concentrated: enough to exit learning on one or two campaigns, typically a few thousand monthly, scaled only when cost per result holds. Kill rules are written before launch.

How does this pair with our Google Ads?

Meta creates and nurtures the demand, Google captures it when evaluators start searching. Run under one measurement spine, each channel makes the other cheaper.
