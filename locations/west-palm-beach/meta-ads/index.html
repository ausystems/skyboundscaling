The audience

In Palm Beach County, Facebook is not the old platform. It is where the money that buys estates, advisory, and medicine spends its evenings.

Write off Facebook here and you write off the demographic that holds the county's wealth. The retirees and semi-retirees who fill the county from November through April check it daily, follow the community groups for their gated neighborhoods, and click ads for things they genuinely intend to buy: physicians, advisors, remodelers, travel, golf. Facebook ads in West Palm Beach reach an audience most brands quit competing for years ago, which is exactly what keeps the auction reasonable.

Instagram carries the other half of the economy. Atlantic Avenue restaurants in Delray Beach fill seatings from the feed, med spas and aesthetic practices sell the after photo, and county real estate sells the walkthrough video to buyers still shoveling snow up north. Instagram advertising in West Palm Beach is where discovery happens; Facebook is where decisions get made. We run both, differently, on purpose.

How Meta gets built for this county

01

Seasonal residents, reached on time

Snowbirds are targetable before they board the plane. We build audiences from travel and interest signals, from engagement with county pages and events, and above all from your own customer lists, because last winter's client is next winter's warmest retargeting audience. Loud creative that thrives on Miami feeds reads as cheap up here. The county responds to polish: real venues, real work, real people, shot well and edited calmly. And the calendar writes the briefs, season openers, charity galas, equestrian weekends in Wellington, tournament Sundays. An ad that knows what week it is outperforms a generic one all winter.

03

Lead forms with instant follow-up

Instant forms make it easy to raise a hand and easier to be forgotten. Every form we run feeds West Palm Beach AI automation that texts back inside a minute, qualifies, and books, because a seasonal resident with no local loyalties hires whoever responds first. It is the same spine as our West Palm Beach lead generation systems.

04

Sequences for slow decisions

A kitchen remodel, a cosmetic procedure, a wealth advisor: nobody here buys those from one ad. We run retargeting sequences that respect the deliberation, the introduction, then the proof, then the reason to talk, so when a long consideration finally ends, it ends with you instead of with whoever showed up last.

The shortcut

Boosting a post feels like advertising the way waving feels like sailing. Meta counts the applause. Your books never see it.

The boost button optimizes for attention, so it dutifully finds people who react, not people who book. There is no conversion event behind it, no lead form, no exclusion of the clients you already serve, no retargeting audience quietly accumulating for the season. County businesses boost their event posts every winter, collect reactions from three states away, and conclude that Meta ads do not work in Palm Beach County. The button failed them; the platform did not.

A real build runs in Ads Manager with outcomes wired in: form submitted, appointment booked, table reserved, order placed. That is also where the actual strategy lives, the split between year-round locals and seasonal arrivals, the exclusions that stop you paying to reach your own clients, the sequences that walk a slow decision forward. None of it is visible from the boost button, which is why the results never are either.

West Palm Beach Meta ads questions, answered

Does Facebook advertising still work in West Palm Beach?

Yes, arguably better than in younger markets. The demographic that holds the county's wealth checks Facebook daily and clicks ads for services it genuinely needs, while many advertisers have chased trends elsewhere and left this auction less crowded. Instagram carries discovery for restaurants, aesthetics, real estate, and events. The two platforms do different jobs here, and we run them differently.

What budget do Meta ads need here?

Most county campaigns start well at $1,500 to $3,000 per month in ad spend, enough to test several creative angles and build retargeting audiences before the season peaks. High-consideration categories like remodeling, medical, and advisory start in the same range and scale once the sequence proves itself. Management is a separate flat fee, published in bands on our pricing page.

Do you produce the ad creative?

Yes, creative is most of the job. We script and edit the video, design the statics and carousels, and write copy in the register this market rewards: polished, specific, calm. From you we need the real thing, your venue, your projects, your people, because in a county saturated with glossy stock advertising, real and well made is what stops the scroll.

Why not just boost posts?

Because boosting optimizes for attention, so Meta finds people who applaud rather than people who book. There is no conversion tracking behind it, no lead form, no exclusion of existing clients, and no retargeting audience accumulating for the season. Boost a post to celebrate news. To acquire customers, run real campaigns in Ads Manager with outcomes wired in.

How do you measure revenue from Meta ads?

By connecting spend to what actually happened. The pixel and Conversions API feed events from the site, lead forms flow into the CRM, and appointments, reservations, and sales get tagged back to the campaign that produced them. Reports lead with cost per booked customer and tracked revenue. Campaigns that win applause but book nothing get cut.
