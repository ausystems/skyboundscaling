The geography

Palm Beach County is one name stretched over many separate markets. A campaign that targets all of it at once pays for strangers it can never serve.

Look at the map before you look at the keywords. A roofer based in Jupiter has no business paying for clicks in Boca Raton, half the county away, yet that is exactly what a default location setting buys. The county runs long, from Tequesta down past Boca Raton and inland to Wellington and Royal Palm Beach, and most PPC agencies treat it as one circle with a pin dropped on West Palm Beach. The budget leaks at both ends: ads showing in cities your trucks will never reach, thin coverage in the neighborhoods that actually book you.

We structure accounts the way the county buys. City-tier campaigns with their own budgets, so Palm Beach Gardens spend cannot cannibalize Boynton Beach. Radius discipline around your real service area instead of a hopeful circle around the whole coast. Bid adjustments that reflect what a customer in each city is worth to you, because downtown West Palm Beach, the gated communities off PGA Boulevard, and the barns of Wellington are different economies. That is the baseline any PPC agency West Palm Beach businesses hire should bring on day one: geography first, keywords second.

Where West Palm Beach ad budgets leak

04 leaks

01

Broad match with the leash off

Left unsupervised, broad match spends a service budget on research queries, DIY questions, and cities on the wrong end of the county. We rebuild around exact and phrase match and read the search terms report weekly, because Google's idea of a related search is generous with your money.

02

No negative keyword discipline

A pool company here pays for job seekers typing pool cleaning jobs and hobbyists typing DIY heater repair until somebody builds the negative list. We maintain negatives per campaign from day one and prune them every week, so the queries that never buy stop billing you.

03

Expensive clicks sent to the homepage

A costly click lands on a homepage about everything you do, scrolls twice, and leaves. Every ad we run points at a dedicated page about the exact service and city it promised. Nothing kills county PPC quietly like a homepage doing a landing page's job.

04

No conversion import, so the account learns nothing

Most accounts optimize toward clicks because nobody told them what booked. We wire call tracking, form events, and CRM outcomes back into Google Ads, so smart bidding learns from revenue instead of curiosity and every report answers the only question that matters: what did this spend book?

The calendar

From November through April this county fills with people who need new vendors immediately. Accounts that scale in October take the season. Accounts that react in January fund them.

Seasonal residents land with no plumber, no dermatologist, no landscaper, and no patience for a long search. They rebuild an entire vendor list through Google in their first weeks back, which makes early November some of the most valuable ad inventory of the year. So we plan the curve in advance: budgets step up through October, campaigns for the services snowbirds need first go live before the season turns, and January's data sharpens February instead of teaching lessons that should have been learned in the fall.

Trades run a different calendar. Roofers, tree crews, and restoration companies press hardest around storm season, when a single forecast can move a month of demand into one week, and generator and impact-window searches ride the same weather. Some categories barely dip at all: medical, legal, and financial searches hold year-round, because the people who moved here full time did not stop needing them in July. The budget curve is set per category, on purpose, before the money moves.

West Palm Beach PPC questions, answered

What budget do Google Ads need in West Palm Beach?

Enough to buy real data in your category and your slice of the county. Most home and marine service accounts do that at $2,000 to $4,000 per month in ad spend before management fees. Estate legal, medical, and financial categories usually need $5,000 and up, because each click costs more and each client is worth far more. We size budgets to the cities you actually serve, never to the whole county by default.

What are your management fees?

A flat monthly fee scoped to the work in the account, published in bands on our pricing page. We do not take a percentage of spend, because a fee that grows with your budget rewards the wrong behavior. You will know the number before the first call ends.

How fast do results come?

Google Ads is the fastest channel we run. West Palm Beach campaigns typically produce their first tracked calls within two to four weeks, then sharpen across the first 90 days as conversion data stacks up. Launching ahead of the November season shortens the ramp, because arriving residents do part of the work. We report against booked revenue from week one and never promise a specific number.

Should we run Local Services Ads or search ads?

Licensed trades and law firms should usually run both. Local Services Ads sit at the top of the page, charge per lead, and convert on your license, insurance, and reviews. Search campaigns cover what LSAs cannot: specific services, specific cities, your own copy, and your own landing pages. Seasonal pushes also live in search, because LSAs cannot be timed the way campaigns can.

Who owns the ad account?

You do, without exception. We build inside your Google Ads account, on your billing, and your admin access never leaves. The campaigns, the negative lists, and every year of conversion history stay with you if we ever part ways. Any agency that keeps the account in its own name is planning for the breakup, not the partnership.

How do you handle the seasonal swing?

With a budget curve set per category, in advance. Household and consumer services step up through October so campaigns are seasoned when seasonal residents arrive in November. Trades hold reserve for storm season, when a single forecast can move a month of demand into a week. Year-round categories like medical and legal stay steady. What we never do is panic-scale in January, when the season's best customers have already chosen.
