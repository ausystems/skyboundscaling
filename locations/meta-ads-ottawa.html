The market

Ottawa deals are won between meetings. Retargeting is how your firm stays in the room when nobody is meeting.

The Ottawa buying cycle has long quiet stretches: after the research, before the shortlist; after the presentation, before the decision. Meta fills those stretches. Retargeting with proof led creative, case outcomes, credentials, and thought leadership, keeps your firm familiar to the exact people who already showed interest, at a cost that rounds to nothing beside the value of one contract.

For firms in Kanata’s tech park and the consulting corridor downtown, it is the most reliable way to fill a room with the right people.

Questions, answered

Does Meta advertising work for B2B in Ottawa?

As the nurture layer, yes: our strongest Meta result, 4.8x ROAS, is a B2B account. The feed is where committee members scroll after work, and familiarity compounds across long cycles.

Can you promote webinars and briefings?

That is a core play here: precise audience targeting for registration, reminders that protect attendance, and retargeting that converts attendees into consultations.

Is Meta appropriate for firms selling to government?

For brand familiarity and event promotion among the professional audience, yes, with conservative, credential led creative. It complements the diligence capture our Google Ads campaigns run.

What does success look like in 90 days?

Audience assets built, retargeting live, events filling, and early pipeline attribution flowing. Long cycles mean revenue verdicts arrive with your sales cycle, and the reporting is honest about that from day one.
