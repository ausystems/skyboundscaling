The market

Markham quietly runs a huge share of Canadian tech. Hundreds of technology firms, from giants to startups, in one corridor.

Markham calls itself Canada’s high tech capital for a reason: national head offices, engineering firms, and product companies line the 404 corridor, with startups filling the floors between them. Alongside tech sits advanced manufacturing and a deep professional services layer serving one of the most diverse, multilingual populations in the country.

Buyers here are technical and skeptical. Engineers, product leads, and founders evaluate your site the way they evaluate a spec sheet: does it load fast, is the information dense where it matters, does the design signal a company that sweats details. A slow template site fails that inspection instantly.

Questions, answered

Can you build websites for technical products?

Yes. Callura, a healthcare AI product, is our flagship example: brand and website built end to end for a technical offering that had to earn trust instantly.

Our team wants to edit the site without developers. Possible?

That is why we build marketing sites on Framer. Your team changes copy, pages, and posts from one editor while the product engineers stay on the product.

Can you help us reach Markham’s multilingual market?

We design for clarity first, so the site communicates across language backgrounds, and we structure builds so localized pages can be added cleanly when you are ready.

Do you work with startups here or only established firms?

Both. Our startup work, like the VAZA pre launch funnel, runs at founder speed, and our pricing bands keep early stage scopes honest.
