The market

Vaughan paid search is a race to the phone call. High value jobs, urgent searches, and the first credible responder wins.

Home services search in Vaughan carries some of the strongest commercial intent in the GTA: renovations, pools and landscaping, custom builds, HVAC and trades, searched by homeowners in Kleinburg, Woodbridge, and Maple with real budgets and real timelines. Click prices reflect it, which punishes sloppy accounts and rewards precise ones.

Precision here means three things: campaigns segmented by service and urgency, ads that mention the areas you actually serve, and a landing experience that produces a call or a booked estimate rather than a brochure visit. Local Services Ads sit alongside standard search for many trades, and the two need to be managed as one system.

Questions, answered

How much do Google Ads cost for contractors in Vaughan?

Home services clicks in the GTA are expensive, often tens of dollars for urgent terms, which is exactly why structure matters: one landed renovation or pool project pays for months of well managed spend.

Should I run Local Services Ads or regular Google Ads?

Usually both, managed as one system: LSA for the trades categories where it applies, standard search for project and research terms that LSA cannot reach.

How fast will my phone ring?

Days, not months. Search captures existing demand, so signal shows in the first weeks. Our 90 day account average is 4.2x ROAS across categories.

Can you track which ads produce actual booked jobs?

Yes: call tracking, form attribution, and job value imports connect revenue back to keywords, so we scale the terms that book estimates, not the ones that only ring the phone.
