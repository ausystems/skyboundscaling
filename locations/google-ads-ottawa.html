The market

Ottawa searches read like an audit trail. Credentials, standards, and vendor comparisons, typed by careful people.

Questions, answered

Do Google Ads work for government contractors?

For the visibility layer, yes: evaluators and program staff research vendors on Google long before any procurement portal opens. Ads put your capability page into that diligence at exactly the right moment.

Should our Ottawa campaigns run in French too?

If you serve federal or bilingual markets, almost certainly. French inventory is often cheaper and less contested, and parallel quality campaigns signal the seriousness Ottawa buyers expect.

How do you measure ads with nine month sales cycles?

Layered reporting: leading indicators now, consultations and qualified pipeline monthly, and CRM imported revenue as cycles close, so the account optimizes toward contracts rather than clicks.

Can remote management work for an Ottawa firm?

It already does: engagements run from Toronto with same day response, and we travel for the milestones that deserve a room. Distance has never decided a campaign.
