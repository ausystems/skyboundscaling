# Entity Corroboration Ladder — 2026-08-19
A realistic progression, with what does and does not count at each level.

### LEVEL 0 — First-party only *(current position)*
- **Status:** ACHIEVED and technically strong
- Evidence: canonical site, clean URLs, complete entity graph with founder, company Instagram, founder Instagram
- **What does NOT count:** the company's own website, its own social accounts, its own schema. None of these corroborate; they only *claim*.
- Why it matters: it is the prerequisite. Claims must exist and be consistent before corroboration has anything to agree with.

### LEVEL 1 — Complete official profile ecosystem *(next, and mostly a single afternoon)*
- **Status:** NOT MET. LinkedIn absent; founder profile incomplete; GBP absent
- Required: LinkedIn company page + founder position; founder IG bio stating role and linking the site; GBP verified (eligibility pending); optionally X
- Best next actions: the four items in `19-priority-action-plan.md` DO FIRST
- **Does NOT count:** creating profiles that sit empty, or profiles whose details contradict the site

### LEVEL 2 — Client / partner corroboration
- **Status:** NOT MET — 0/7
- Required: 2–3 client footer credits linking the canonical URL; consented testimonials from agency partners
- Why it matters: first *independent* domains, topically relevant, and evidence the company does what it claims
- **Does NOT count:** reciprocal link swaps, paid placements, or credits on sites the company itself controls

### LEVEL 3 — Business databases and directories
- **Status:** UNKNOWN (403s) → verify then build
- Required: Crunchbase record; Clutch profile with **real verified** client reviews; optionally one quality agency directory; Bing Places
- **Does NOT count:** bulk directory submissions, or any directory that exists to sell links

### LEVEL 4 — Independent founder / company references
- **Status:** NOT MET
- Required: quotes in journalist-sourced articles; a speaker/member page; any third-party page stating "Ahmad Khalid, Founder of Skybound Scaling"
- **Does NOT count:** self-published articles, or bios the company wrote on platforms it controls

### LEVEL 5 — Editorial / podcast / event coverage
- **Status:** NOT MET
- Required: 1–2 podcast interviews with show-note links; a contributed piece in a real industry publication; local business media
- **Does NOT count:** pay-to-publish "press releases", sponsored posts, or guest-post marketplaces

### LEVEL 6 — Diverse multi-source entity recognition *(the goal)*
- **Status:** NOT MET
- Looks like: the same five facts stated by 8–12 independent sources across 5+ source types, at which point brand queries return the company rather than Skybound Realty or Skybound Entertainment
- **Does NOT count:** volume of low-quality mentions. Diversity and agreement matter far more than count.

**Realistic sequencing:** Levels 1 and 2 are achievable in ~30 days and carry most of the value. Level 3 follows within 60. Levels 4–5 accrue over 90+ days and cannot be rushed without resorting to the tactics this project prohibits.
