## Ordering rationale
The three CRITICAL platforms are the ones that simultaneously (a) assert the category, (b) assert the founder relationship, and (c) are heavily retrieved for company-identity questions. Crunchbase and Clutch follow because they are structured databases that other services and AI systems reuse. Everything below MEDIUM is optional and should never be pursued in bulk: ten consistent records beat a hundred inconsistent ones.
