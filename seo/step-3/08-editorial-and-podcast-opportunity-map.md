## Why source-request platforms rank first
They are the only channel here that converts *existing* expertise into an *independent* citation without needing an audience, a milestone, or a relationship. Each accepted quote produces a third-party page stating "Ahmad Khalid, Founder of Skybound Scaling" — which is exactly the sentence the entity is missing everywhere on the public web.

## Non-negotiables
Never pay for editorial. Decline any outlet charging to publish "news". Use the identical bio from the consistency pack every time so mentions compound into one entity instead of scattering.
