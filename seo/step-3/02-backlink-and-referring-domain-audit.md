# Backlink & Referring Domain Audit — 2026-08-19

## Methodology and its limits (stated first, deliberately)
No commercial backlink index (Ahrefs / Semrush / Majestic) is authenticated in this environment; the Ahrefs connector requires an OAuth flow the owner has not completed. This audit therefore observes: search-visible citations, direct rendered-DOM inspection of the seven most likely linking domains (the clients), and platform probes. That covers the links that would matter for entity corroboration, but it is **not** a complete referring-domain enumeration.
**Anything below marked INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE should be re-run once a commercial index is connected.**

## The five questions
1. **Domain diversity:** 1 domain. Effectively none.
2. **Source diversity:** one source *type* (owned social). Zero of the six other categories.
3. **Entity corroboration from links:** none. No link independently asserts what the company is.
4. **Brand-anchor profile:** no anchor-text profile exists. Nothing over-optimised — a clean starting position.
5. **Biggest missing category:** **client attribution links** (Type B). Seven real, topically-relevant, independently-owned domains exist and none links back. This is the largest available gain at the lowest cost and highest legitimacy.

## Honest assessment
For a company founded in 2025 this is not alarming, but it is genuinely the binding constraint. There is no link-based reason for any engine to believe the entity exists beyond its own website. The fix is not volume — 5–8 genuine referring domains across 4 different source types would transform this profile.
