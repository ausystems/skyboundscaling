## Methodology rules
Re-run the **same** queries from `11-ai-and-search-retrieval-baseline.md` so results are comparable. Record the date and the search surface every time. Treat one search backend as one observation, not universal truth. Never interpret an AI product's answer as a measurement of its ranking system. Distinguish *not found* from *does not exist*, and *not retrieved* from *not indexed*.
