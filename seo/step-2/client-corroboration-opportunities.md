## Ground rules (keep it legitimate)
- Ask; never require. A small visible footer line ("Website by Skybound Scaling") with a normal link is standard industry attribution, not a link scheme.
- Never ask white-label partners to disclose anything they contractually or commercially keep private.
- No sitewide-footer link *networks*, no keyword-stuffed anchors — the anchor should be the brand name, nothing else.
- Two or three credits from these domains would constitute the company's **first independent, topically-relevant, non-self-published corroboration** — the highest-value external signal available at zero cost.
