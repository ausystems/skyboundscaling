Questions, answered

How much does a website cost in Toronto?

It depends on scope. Our brief form uses budget bands from under $10k to $75k and up, and we will tell you inside one call which band your project actually needs. No inflated scope, no surprise line items.

How long does a website take to build?

A focused marketing site can go from brief to live in three to six weeks. Our fastest launch shipped in 18 days. Timelines move with content readiness, so we help you prepare copy and assets early.

Do you build on Framer or custom code?

Both. Framer is our default for marketing sites because it ships fast, edits easily, and performs well. We move to custom code when integrations, products, or scale require it.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. Every build ends with a handover session and a site structured so your team can change copy, images, and pages without a developer.

Do you handle SEO during the build?

Every site ships with technical SEO in place: clean structure, fast load, schema markup, sitemaps, and redirects. Ongoing rankings work is available through our SEO service.
