The revenue leak

Most businesses do not lose revenue to weak marketing. They lose it to slow follow-up.

A lead that waits an hour is usually a lead that already called someone else. The first few minutes decide who gets the conversation, yet most companies still answer inquiries between jobs, after dinner, or the next morning. That gap is the leak, and it swallows ad spend silently. Lead follow-up automation closes it: every inquiry from your website, your ads, or your Google Business Profile gets a response in seconds, gets qualified, and gets offered a time on your calendar. This layer sits underneath our lead generation systems and Google Ads management for a reason. We sell complete customer acquisition systems, and automation is the part that stops them leaking.

What we build

07 systems

01

Speed-to-lead response

Every new inquiry gets a text or email back within seconds, any hour, any day. The system opens the conversation while your competitors are still checking voicemail, so you are first in line instead of fifth.

02

AI answering and chat

An assistant trained on your services, prices, and policies handles questions on your site and inbound messages, qualifies the lead, and passes the thread to a human cleanly the moment one is needed.

Appointment booking and reminders

Qualified leads pick a slot on a synced calendar without waiting for a callback. Confirmations and reminders go out automatically, so no-shows stop quietly draining your week.

Nurture sequences

Leads that are not ready today get useful follow-up on a schedule until they are. Written in your voice, sent at sane intervals, and stopped the instant they reply or book.

05

Pipeline automation and lead routing

New leads land in the right stage, assigned to the right person, with the next task already created. CRM automation keeps deals moving without a manager pushing them by hand.

06

Internal ops automations

Quotes, invoices, review requests, job status updates, and handoffs between tools. The repetitive admin your team does between sales, done by the system instead of a person.

07

Reporting dashboards

One screen showing leads in, response times, booked calls, show rates, and revenue by source. You see exactly what the machine is doing and exactly what it is producing.

How a build runs

04 steps

01

Audit the leaks

We trace every path a lead can take into your business and time how long each one waits for an answer. Most audits find money sitting in unanswered messages within the first hour.

02

Map the flows

Every automation gets designed on paper first: triggers, messages, branches, and the exact moments a human takes over. You approve the map before we build anything.

03

Build and launch

We wire the flows into your stack, load your voice and your answers, test every branch against real scenarios, and go live in stages, starting with speed to lead because it pays back first.

04

Monitor and tune

Automations are not fire and forget. We read transcripts, catch weak answers, tighten routing, and expand what works, so the system gets sharper every month it runs.

Questions, answered

Which tools and CRMs do you work with?

Almost any of them. HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive, Zoho, and Salesforce are the common ones, connected through native automations, Make, Zapier, n8n, or custom code. If you have no CRM yet, we install one that fits your team size and budget, then build the automation into it from day one.

Is AI safe to put in front of my customers?

Yes, with guardrails. The assistant answers only from approved information about your services, prices, and policies, and it escalates to a person the moment a conversation calls for one. You approve how it sounds before it ever speaks to a lead, and we review transcripts to tighten weak answers.

What does AI automation cost?

It scopes to the number of flows and integrations. Most builds run as a one-time project with a light monthly retainer for monitoring and improvements, and our pricing page shows the typical bands. If a simpler setup would serve you fine, we will say so on the first call.

How long does a build take?

Speed-to-lead flows typically go live inside the first two weeks, because that is where the fastest payback sits. A full build with booking, nurture, routing, and reporting usually lands in three to six weeks, depending on how many tools we are connecting and how clean your data is.

What happens when the AI cannot answer something?

It stops guessing. The conversation routes to you or your team instantly, the lead is told a person is stepping in, and the miss gets logged so the assistant learns the answer for next time. Nothing dead-ends, and nothing gets invented.
