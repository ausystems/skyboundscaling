Questions, answered

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

Enough for the data to teach us something: usually a few thousand dollars monthly minimum in competitive niches. We size the starting budget to your economics on the first call.

What is your management fee?

Flat retainer scoped to account complexity, from the lower bands of our brief form. No percentage of spend, so our incentive is your return, not your budget.

How fast will I see results?

Signal in the first two weeks, meaningful ROAS readings by weeks four to six once conversion data accumulates. Our 90 day average across accounts is 4.2x.

Do you build the landing pages too?

Yes, through our landing page service. Ads and pages built as one system convert measurably better than ads pointed at a generic homepage.

Google Ads or Meta ads first?

Google captures demand that already exists, Meta creates it. Tight budget with active search volume: start Google. Building category awareness: start Meta. Many clients graduate to both.
