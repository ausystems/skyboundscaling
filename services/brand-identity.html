Questions, answered

What does a brand identity project include?

Positioning, logo suite, color palette, typography, visual system, usage guidelines, and production files. Most clients pair it with a website so the system launches somewhere real.

How long does brand identity take?

A focused identity system takes three to five weeks. Paired with a website, the combined engagement typically runs six to nine weeks from brief to launch.

How much does branding cost?

Identity work usually lands in the lower bands of our brief form, which runs from under $10k to $75k and up. Scope moves with deliverables, not with hours padding.

Can you refresh an existing brand instead of replacing it?

Yes. Refreshes keep the equity you have earned and fix consistency, hierarchy, and application. We tell you honestly which path your brand needs.

Do we own the final files?

Fully. Source files, fonts licensing guidance, and every export format, delivered and documented. No hostage assets.
