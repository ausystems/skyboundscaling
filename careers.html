What working here means

03 truths

01

You own the work

No layers between you and the client. You scope it, you ship it, you answer for it, and you get the credit when it lands.

02

Craft is the culture

We ship websites finished to the standard of luxury homes and ad accounts tuned like portfolios. Good enough is not a phrase we use.

03

Numbers over noise

No performative standups, no vanity decks. The work is measured against revenue and the calendar is protected for deep work.
