Work · Opinion platform

VAZA.

VAZA is an opinion-based platform where users vote on matchups across sports, entertainment, music, anime, and pop culture. Players can vote for free or back their take, with payouts based on the final results and how close the matchup runs.

The scope

End to end

Lead-capture landing page

A single conversion-focused landing page built to turn pre-launch attention into signups, with one message, one action, and no leaks.

Pre-launch funnel

The journey from first click to confirmed lead, engineered step by step so early believers don't slip away between touchpoints.

Backend automation

The backend that captures, organizes, and automates the entire lead flow, so every signup is routed and followed up without manual work.

The brief

Ahead of launch, VAZA needed to turn attention into an audience and capture early believers, keep them organized, and stay in front of them automatically.

The work

We built the landing page and the funnel behind it, then wired the backend so every lead is captured, sorted, and followed up around the clock.
